# Zyngram Enterprise Services

## 1. Core Service Portfolio
Zyngram delivers four core product offerings designed for scalable supply chain acceleration and digital asset logistics:

### 1.1 Zyngram Express Transit
A guaranteed time-critical logistics service leveraging the Point-Center-Hub network to achieve sub-4-hour intra-city deliveries and same-day inter-city linehaul transitions. Features live GPS telemetry and automated milestone verification.

### 1.2 Zyngram Nexus Cloud
An enterprise hybrid cloud pipeline offering distributed telemetry ingestion across digital Nodes, micro-fulfillment inventory synchronization, and zero-downtime routing failovers.

### 1.3 Zynora RAG Intelligence System
An internal AI-powered Retrieval-Augmented Generation assistant engineered to provide staff and franchise operators with verified, grounded, and policy-compliant operational answers without exposing enterprise records to external cloud LLM providers.

### 1.4 Zyngram Secure Ledger
A tamper-proof operational ledger tracking chain-of-custody handoffs, franchise compliance audits, and delivery confirmation cryptographic signatures across all physical and digital tiers.
